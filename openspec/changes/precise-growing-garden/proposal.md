## Why
Careful touch release and keyboard shifts can select an unintended column because snapping ignores piece-center offsets. Competing tap surfaces can also rotate during an upward send. Runs should continue beyond the first thirty rings and retain both milestone and survival records.

## What Changes
- Apply a modest uniform zoom while retaining cylinder aspect and angles.
- Snap to the selected occupied-grid anchor; discrete keyboard shifts visit each adjacent reachable column. Reserve coast for deliberate flicks.
- Classify each touch exclusively as tap, turn or upward send. Pulse corresponding borderless rotation icons for side taps; retain the ascent button contour.
- Keep first thirty-ring time, continue until blocked spawn, and show 30 Rings, Rings and Monstera values from each recorded run in time/rings rankings.
- Enlarge incoming previews. Grow individually seeded leaves through emergence and opening, with larger brighter leaves from multi-ring clears.
