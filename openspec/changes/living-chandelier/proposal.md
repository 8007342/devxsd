# Proposal

## Why
The chandelier garden needs richer living detail while preserving fast, legible play. Wide cells and long inertial settling currently weaken the Tetris feel.

## What Changes
- Add a layered ornamental chandelier, fast trailing pothos, roots, age-driven dirt and moss, and sparse garden insects.
- Render square cell faces with consistent solid geometry for active and settled pieces.
- Interrupt residual spin momentum with prompt, exact collision-safe column snapping.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
None.

### Modified Capabilities
- `cylindrical-puzzle`: botanical presentation, square proportions and quick column snapping.

## Impact
Flame projection and procedural drawing, puzzle inertia and placement ages, model/render tests and art direction. No leaderboard or dependency changes.
